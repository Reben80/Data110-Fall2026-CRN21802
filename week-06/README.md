# Week 6 — Oct 7

**DATA 110: Data Visualization and Communication**

## Topics

- **Ethics in visualization** — Accurate representation, omitted context, and audience trust.
- **Misleading graphs** — Truncated axes, cherry-picked series, and decorative distortion.
- **Correlation is not causation** — Spurious patterns and overclaimed stories.
- **Many distributions at once** — Box plots, violin plots, and ridgeline plots.
