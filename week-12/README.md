# Week 12 — Nov 18

**DATA 110: Data Visualization and Communication**

## Topics

- **Storytelling with data** — A narrative with a beginning, evidence, and a takeaway.
- **Audience** — What a classmate, a manager, or a public reader needs to see first.
- **Clarity** — Titles that state the finding, captions that explain the “so what,” and annotation that points to the claim.
- **Final-project framing** — Choosing a dataset and a question that can support a short visual story.
