# Week 10 — Nov 4

**DATA 110: Data Visualization and Communication**

## Topics

- **Maps as data graphics** — Spatial patterns that tables and bar charts cannot show well.
- **Interactive geographic visualization** — Plotly and related Python mapping tools.
- **Heatmaps and point maps** — Density, location, and the difference between counts and rates.
- **Historical example** — John Snow's 1854 cholera map as a spatial argument.

## Resources / assignment

- Plotly / Folium map lab (posted here and on Blackboard)
- Local open-data sources used in previous terms include Montgomery County, Baltimore, and Washington, DC crime or arrest files. The datasets for this term will be posted with the lab.

Maps make claims about place. Label what is being counted, watch for population differences, and do not treat a hotspot as a complete explanation.
