# Week 10 — Nov 4

**DATA 110: Data Visualization and Communication**

## Topics

Two lectures. Both are in Python, with pandas and matplotlib.

- **Visualizing proportions** — A proportion is a part of a whole. A pie, a stacked bar, and side-by-side bars show the same parts and make different comparisons easy.
- **One whole, three ways** — The 1976–1980 German parliament (243, 214, and 39 seats), made-up market shares for five companies, and the share of seats held by women and men in Rwanda from 1997 to 2016.
- **A proportion inside a proportion** — A mosaic of the penguin data. Column width is the species. The islands split the column.
- **Getting to know your data** — Look at a file before you trust a chart. Accuracy, precision, a data dictionary, and values that are not real measurements.
- **Births in North Carolina, 2001** — 1,450 rows in `NCbirths.csv`. A blank does not change a mean. The number −999 in `Weeks` does. Two race columns in the same file do not say the same thing.
